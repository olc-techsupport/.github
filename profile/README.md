# Welcome to Oglala Lakota College
## Integrated Data Cube Project

Welcome to our community of learners, researchers, and collaborators.
Whether you are taking a class, participating in an internship, conducting
research, or exploring a community question, we invite you to explore
our learning materials, datasets, and environmental data science tools.

The USDA-NIFA Integrated Data Cube (IDC) project at Oglala Lakota College
connects Earth data science with Tribal priorities, knowledge systems,
and self-determined decision-making. Together, we are building resources
to explore land, water, agriculture, and ecosystems while expanding
local expertise in working with environmental data.

- **Interested in collaborating?** Contact us to discuss questions,

## Our Project

Environmental changes affect water availability, habitats, agriculture,
and community well-being. Tribal Nations bring generations of knowledge
and enduring relationships with their homelands to understanding and
responding to these changes.

The Integrated Data Cube initiative brings environmental datasets and
analysis tools together so that students, faculty, and collaborators
can investigate questions across places and through time. Our work
with IDC/Cubedynamics supports both research and hands-on learning
at Oglala Lakota College.

We aim to:

- Expand access to environmental data and tools for Tribal-led research.
- Build local skills in data analysis, mapping, and interpretation.
- Support learning across classes, internships, and research projects.
- Develop workflows that respect Tribal Knowledges and governance.
- Support environmental decisions guided by community priorities.

## Tribal Data Sovereignty and Governance

Tribal sovereignty, data governance, and community leadership guide this work.
Respect for Tribal Knowledges includes respecting decisions about
whether, how, and with whom knowledge is shared.

When working with these resources, review the source documentation,
permissions, attribution requirements, and limitations of each dataset.
Questions about appropriate data use or sharing should be discussed
with the project team and relevant data stewards.
